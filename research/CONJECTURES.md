# Conjecture and theorem ledger

Every item must have a status, assumptions, falsification plan, and promotion criterion.

## B0 — Exact ABA type count

**Status:** DERIVED; paper proof complete; finite exact checks complete; Lean pending.

For the theory of atomless Boolean algebras,

`|T_k| = 2^(2^k) - 1`.

**Mechanism:** a complete k-type is exactly a nonempty zero/nonzero support pattern on the `2^k` Boolean minterm cells.

**Falsification:** find two tuples with the same cell support but different first-order type, or a nonempty support pattern not realizable in ABA. Quantifier elimination and recursive atomless splitting rule these out in the paper proof.

**Promotion:** Lean formalization of at least the combinatorial layer; preferably the ABA QE layer as well.

## B1 — Specification-algebra partition theorem

**Status:** DERIVED finite-algebra lemma; formal proof pending.

Let `T` be a finite type space and let `Delta={delta_1,...,delta_d}` be definable predicates, each identified with a subset of `T`. Define the evaluation map

`e_Delta : T -> {0,1}^d`

by the truth vector of the predicates. Then the Boolean algebra `B_Delta` generated by Delta consists exactly of unions of fibers of `e_Delta`. Its atoms are the nonempty fibers, so if

`r_Delta = |image(e_Delta)|`,

then

`|B_Delta| = 2^r_Delta`,

with

`r_Delta <= min(|T|, 2^d)`.

**Mechanism:** predicates can distinguish two types iff their truth vectors differ. Boolean closure cannot split a truth-vector fiber and can form every union of fibers.

**Falsification:** finite exhaustive search over arbitrary finite T and predicate families; look for a generated Boolean set that is not a union of fibers or a union of fibers not generated by minterms in Delta.

**Relevance:** this is the exact finite-algebra object behind ocLTL §5.2.

## B2 — Exact ocLTL feature quotient

**Status:** CONJECTURE / definition under test.

For the three-variable presentation `(m,x,y)`, define a feature map on `T_3` by

`q_Delta(tau) = ( tau|_(m,x), shift(tau)|_m, truth_Delta(tau) )`.

Conjecture: the image of `q_Delta` is exactly the finite compatibility information needed to reconstruct Asor's §5.2 feasibility constraint, and two complete 3-types with the same feature signature are interchangeable in that translation.

**Important limitation:** this is a claim about that particular propositional translation, not a global minimality theorem for synthesis strategies.

**Falsification:** construct a pair of 3-types with equal feature signatures that force different behavior in the translated LTL formula or in witness reconstruction.

**Promotion:** proof by tracing every occurrence of `Q_tau` in the reduction and the reconstruction proof.

## B3 — Omega-categorical fixed-point elimination baseline

**Status:** DERIVED baseline theorem; novelty not claimed.

Let M be omega-categorical and let `Phi` be a monotone first-order-definable operator on k-ary relations, with the relation variable occurring positively. Then its least and greatest fixed points are parameter-free first-order definable.

Moreover, under the identification of definable k-ary relations with subsets of `T_k`, Kleene iteration stabilizes after at most `|T_k|` strict changes.

**Mechanism:** `T_k` is finite; the operator acts monotonically on the finite lattice `P(T_k)`.

**Falsification:** check assumptions carefully. In particular, allowing parameters, non-positive occurrences, or an operator that is not first-order definable changes the statement.

**Promotion:** formal statement with all syntactic/effectivity hypotheses made explicit.

## C1 — Direct ABA safety synthesis without explicit type enumeration

**Status:** CONJECTURE (algorithmic).

For a fixed-lookback safety specification over ABA, the standard greatest-fixed-point winning-region computation can be implemented by repeated substitution plus ABA quantifier elimination/equivalence checking, without explicitly constructing `T_k`.

The algorithm must terminate by B3. The research question is whether the symbolic formulas remain materially smaller than explicit type-space representations on important families.

**Falsification:** produce a family where every reasonable normalized formula necessarily expands to essentially the full type algebra, or where Tau's available QE interface cannot preserve the required predicate-variable substitution semantics.

**Experiment:** compare formula DAG size, runtime, and peak memory against the explicit support-pattern engine.

## C2 — ABA support-pattern engine is an exact oracle for bounded arity

**Status:** CONJECTURE (engineering exactness; mathematical core follows B0).

For quantifier-free Boolean equations/inequations in k variables, evaluation on a complete ABA k-type can be computed solely from its support bitmask. Restriction maps and the ocLTL `(m,x,y)` compatibility maps can also be computed solely by bit operations.

**Falsification:** differential-test against Tau's Boolean-algebra solver on exhaustively generated small formulas and all k-types for small k.

**Promotion:** zero divergences over a generated bounded grammar plus a proof that the grammar compiler preserves minterm semantics.

## C3 — Useful compression is governed by realized signatures, not |Delta| alone

**Status:** CONJECTURE (complexity/empirical).

The practical size of the §5.2 abstraction is better predicted by `r_Delta = |image(e_Delta)|` and by the number of realized compatibility signatures than by the raw number of predicates `|Delta|`.

**Falsification:** benchmark families where `r_Delta` is small but the construction remains large for a reason not explained by temporal LTL size or compatibility structure.

**Potential theorem direction:** parameterized complexity in `r_Delta`, orbit growth, lookback, and stream count.

---

## C4 — Common finiteness principle behind ocLTL and CSL 2026

**Status:** OPEN-COMPARISON; do not assume true.

Investigate whether there is a useful general abstraction theorem subsuming both:

1. finite complete-type/orbit spaces from effective omega-categoricity; and
2. effective omega-regular satisfiability / omega-regular approximability of constraint sequences from CSL 2026.

**Falsification:** first seek separation examples. `(N,<)` already shows that omega-categoricity is not necessary for the CSL 2026 route.

**Questions:**

- Does effective omega-categoricity imply effective omega-regular satisfiability for an appropriate bounded constraint encoding?
- If not, what additional completion/homogeneity assumption is missing?
- Can omega-regular satisfiability hold for domains with infinite k-orbit spaces?
- Is there a categorical/automata object that both finite type abstraction and constraint-sequence automata instantiate?

## C5 — Decidability boundary for memory

**Status:** OPEN PROGRAM.

Map the exact boundary among bounded lookback, bounded registers, restricted recurrence, and unbounded freeze/register mechanisms.

**Known warning:** freeze LTL and unbounded register synthesis contain strong undecidability results; ocLTL deliberately fixes lookback; CSL 2026 deliberately fixes the number of registers.

**Goal:** identify a strictly more expressive memory discipline than fixed lookback for at least one rich theory while retaining decidable synthesis.

**Falsification strategy:** attempt counter-machine encodings early, before proof construction.

---

## C6 — Myhill-Nerode-style residual for synthesis

**Status:** QUARANTINED CONJECTURE.

The MFCS 2025 canonical/minimal register-automata results motivate asking whether a useful residual equivalence exists for infinite-data reactive synthesis. No candidate definition is currently accepted.

**Do not use** ordinary language residual equivalence without addressing the alternating environment/system quantifier structure and strategy dependence.

**First task:** derive the finite-alphabet analogue for deterministic reactive games and identify exactly what object is canonical there. Then lift equivariantly, if possible.

**Kill condition:** if even the finite-alphabet target lacks the desired canonical minimality under the chosen implementation model, abandon this line rather than forcing the analogy.

## C7 — Stronger-than-counting structure of ABA type maps

**Status:** OPEN PROGRAM.

Because ABA k-types are nonempty supports on the Boolean cube `{0,1}^k`, all coordinate restriction maps are induced by cube projections. Explore the resulting combinatorics:

- fiber sizes of restriction maps;
- extension counts;
- incidence matrices between `T_k` and `T_{k+1}`;
- Möbius/zeta transforms on support families;
- symmetry under coordinate permutations and bit-complement actions;
- compressed representations (BDD/ZDD/antichain forms) for sets of types.

**Goal:** find a representation whose operations match synthesis predecessor/fixed-point operations more directly than DNF/CNF.

**Noether criterion:** seek the representation in which the operation becomes structurally natural, not merely faster on examples.
