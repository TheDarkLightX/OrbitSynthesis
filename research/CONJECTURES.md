# Conjecture and theorem ledger

Every item must have a status, assumptions, falsification plan, and promotion criterion.

## B0 — Exact ABA type count

**Status:** DERIVED; paper proof complete; finite exact checks complete; Lean pending.

For the theory of atomless Boolean algebras,

`|T_k| = 2^(2^k) - 1`.

**Mechanism:** a complete k-type is exactly a nonempty zero/nonzero support pattern on the `2^k` Boolean minterm cells.

**Falsification:** find two tuples with the same cell support but different first-order type, or a nonempty support pattern not realizable in ABA. Quantifier elimination and recursive atomless splitting rule these out in the paper proof.

**Promotion:** Lean formalization of at least the combinatorial layer; preferably the ABA QE layer as well.

## B1 — Specification-algebra partition theorem

**Status:** DERIVED finite-algebra lemma; formal proof pending.

Let `T` be a finite type space and let `Delta={delta_1,...,delta_d}` be definable predicates, each identified with a subset of `T`. Define the evaluation map

`e_Delta : T -> {0,1}^d`

by the truth vector of the predicates. Then the Boolean algebra `B_Delta` generated by Delta consists exactly of unions of fibers of `e_Delta`. Its atoms are the nonempty fibers, so if

`r_Delta = |image(e_Delta)|`,

then

`|B_Delta| = 2^r_Delta`,

with

`r_Delta <= min(|T|, 2^d)`.

**Mechanism:** predicates can distinguish two types iff their truth vectors differ. Boolean closure cannot split a truth-vector fiber and can form every union of fibers.

**Falsification:** finite exhaustive search over arbitrary finite T and predicate families; look for a generated Boolean set that is not a union of fibers or a union of fibers not generated by minterms in Delta.

**Relevance:** this is the exact finite-algebra object behind ocLTL §5.2.

## B2 — Exact ocLTL feature quotient

**Status:** CONJECTURE / definition under test.

For the three-variable presentation `(m,x,y)`, define a feature map on `T_3` by

`q_Delta(tau) = ( tau|_(m,x), shift(tau)|_m, truth_Delta(tau) )`.

Conjecture: the image of `q_Delta` is exactly the finite compatibility information needed to reconstruct Asor's §5.2 feasibility constraint, and two complete 3-types with the same feature signature are interchangeable in that translation.

**Important limitation:** this is a claim about that particular propositional translation, not a global minimality theorem for synthesis strategies.

**Falsification:** construct a pair of 3-types with equal feature signatures that force different behavior in the translated LTL formula or in witness reconstruction.

**Promotion:** proof by tracing every occurrence of `Q_tau` in the reduction and the reconstruction proof.

## B3 — Omega-categorical fixed-point elimination baseline

**Status:** DERIVED baseline theorem; novelty not claimed.

Let M be omega-categorical and let `Phi` be a monotone first-order-definable operator on k-ary relations, with the relation variable occurring positively. Then its least and greatest fixed points are parameter-free first-order definable.

Moreover, under the identification of definable k-ary relations with subsets of `T_k`, Kleene iteration stabilizes after at most `|T_k|` strict changes.

**Mechanism:** `T_k` is finite; the operator acts monotonically on the finite lattice `P(T_k)`.

**Falsification:** check assumptions carefully. In particular, allowing parameters, non-positive occurrences, or an operator that is not first-order definable changes the statement.

**Promotion:** formal statement with all syntactic/effectivity hypotheses made explicit.

## B4 — Atomic ABA predicates are generalized group tests

**Status:** DERIVED exact equivalence; literature connection verified; Lean pending.

For a complete ABA support `S` and a Boolean term with minterm support `A_f`,

`f=0  iff  S intersect A_f = empty`,

and

`f!=0 iff S intersect A_f != empty`.

Thus selecting atomic zero/nonzero equations to distinguish a finite candidate family of complete types is exactly generalized non-adaptive group testing on that set-system.

Equivalently, if each minterm cell receives a d-bit codeword `c(v)`, the signature of a support is the coordinatewise OR

`sig(S)=OR_{v in S} c(v)`.

**Complexity consequence:** the explicit-candidate optimization problem inherits NP-hardness from generalized group testing; membership in NP gives NP-completeness for the decision form.

**Mechanism:** atomic equations can observe only whether their chosen minterm set intersects the nonzero support. This is a Boolean join-semilattice constraint, not an arbitrary information bit.

## B5 — Co-atom obstruction / exact atomic-test complexity

**Status:** DERIVED; finite exhaustive verification complete for the first ocLTL fibers; Lean pending.

Let a candidate support family `E` on finite ground set `U` contain `U` and every co-atom `U\{v}`. Then any exact intersection-query identification scheme, even adaptive, has worst-case depth at least `|U|`; singleton queries attain the bound.

**Proof mechanism:** on the all-positive transcript for unknown `U`, the only query that distinguishes `U` from `U\{v}` is the singleton `{v}`.

For the ABA ocLTL interior-y fiber with r active `(m,x)` rows:

`|F_r|=3^r-2`,

but exact atomic equation complexity is

`2r`  for `r>=2`.

Hence the 79-state hardest fiber needs exactly 8 atomic tests although unrestricted definable predicates need only 7 bits.

## B6 — Multipartite projection-fiber dichotomy

**Status:** DERIVED; small finite cases checked; Lean pending.

Split variables into blocks whose fixed coarse type supports have sizes `r_1,...,r_b`. A fine ABA type in the projection fiber is exactly a subset

`S subseteq R_1 x ... x R_b`

whose coordinate projection onto every `R_i` is surjective: a spanning b-partite b-uniform hypergraph with no isolated vertex.

The exact fiber cardinality is given by inclusion-exclusion:

`F(r_1,...,r_b)
 = sum_{a_1,...,a_b}
   (-1)^(sum a_i)
   * product_i binom(r_i,a_i)
   * 2^(product_i (r_i-a_i)).`

Atomic identification has a sharp dichotomy:

- if at most one `r_i>1`, the fiber is a singleton: 0 tests;
- if at least two `r_i>1`, every fine-cell singleton is forced: exactly `product_i r_i` tests.

**Mechanism:** in the nondegenerate case the complete hypergraph and every one-edge deletion remain valid members of the fiber.

## B7 — Support Booleanization / propositional ABA projection

**Status:** DERIVED semantic theorem; reference ROBDD implementation and randomized bounded differential checks complete; Tau differential testing and Lean pending.

Represent a k-variable ABA type by `2^k` Boolean support bits. Quantifier-free ABA formulas translate to ordinary Boolean formulas over those bits.

Adding one new BA variable has exact extension relation

`Ext_k(z,w)=AND_v [ z_v <-> (w_(v,0) OR w_(v,1)) ].`

Therefore

`hat(exists y. phi)(z)
 = exists w. [Ext_k(z,w) AND hat(phi)(w)]`

on valid coarse supports.

**Mechanism:** each nonzero coarse minterm can be split independently into left-only, right-only, or both; every such finite splitting exists by atomlessness.

**Promotion:** differential-test against Tau over generated formulas and formalize the extension-realization lemma.

## B8 — Exact linear ROBDD for ABA extension

**Status:** DERIVED; reference ROBDD exact counts checked for up to 16 coarse cells; paper proof complete; formal BDD proof pending.

Under cell-local variable order

`z_1,w_10,w_11,z_2,w_20,w_21,...`,

the reduced ordered BDD for an n-cell ABA one-variable extension relation has exactly

`5n`

nonterminal nodes.

For k retained BA variables this is

`5*2^k`.

**Mechanism:** every local relation `z <-> (a OR b)` contributes exactly five unreduced nodes, and the independent later blocks splice into the accepting terminals without causing cross-variable merges.

**Caveat:** this proves compactness of the extension relation, not arbitrary formula BDDs.

## B9 — Exact type geometry with finitely many interpreted constants

**Status:** DERIVED; standard QE ingredients; computational exploitation new research target; Lean pending.

Let `C` be a finite set of interpreted constants in an atomless BA, and let

`rho(C)`

be the number of nonzero atoms of the finite Boolean subalgebra generated by C. Then

`|T_k(C)| = (2^(2^k)-1)^rho(C)`.

A support representation uses exactly

`rho(C)*2^k`

bits, with at least one active variable-minterm bit in every nonzero constant region. The one-variable extension relation has a natural ROBDD with exactly

`5*rho(C)*2^k`

nonterminal nodes.

**Mechanism:** every nonzero constant region is itself atomless in the ambient BA and can be refined independently by the variable Venn cells.

**Parameter insight:** `rho(C)` may be exponential in `|C|` in generic position but only linear for nested/disjoint/partition-like constant families.

## C1 — Direct ABA safety synthesis without explicit type enumeration

**Status:** CONJECTURE (algorithmic), strengthened by B7/B8.

For a fixed-lookback safety specification over ABA, compute the greatest-fixed-point winning region directly as a Boolean DAG over support bits, using local extension relations plus Boolean abstraction for BA quantifiers, without explicit construction of the complete type space.

Semantic termination follows from B3; B7 provides exact projection semantics; B8 proves the extension relation itself is compact.

**Falsification:** produce a natural Tau formula family whose support-BDD fixed-point iterates necessarily blow up to the explicit type-scale, or show that temporal/input-output quantifier structure destroys the local projection advantage.

**Experiment:** compare formula DAG size, runtime, and peak memory against explicit support/type enumeration and Tau's current QE path.

## C2 — ABA support-pattern engine is an exact oracle for bounded arity

**Status:** CONJECTURE (engineering exactness; mathematical core follows B0/B7).

For Boolean equations/inequations in k variables, evaluation on a complete ABA k-type can be computed solely from its support bitmask. Restriction, extension, and quantifier projection can likewise be computed by support operations.

**Falsification:** differential-test against Tau's Boolean-algebra solver on exhaustively generated small formulas and all k-types for small k.

**Promotion:** zero divergences over a generated bounded grammar plus a proof that the grammar compiler preserves minterm semantics.

## C3 — Useful compression is governed by semantic ranks, not raw syntax counts

**Status:** CONJECTURE (complexity/empirical), refined.

Practical symbolic complexity is better predicted by semantic parameters such as:

- `r_Delta = |image(e_Delta)|` / realized specification signatures;
- `rho(C)` / nonzero constant partition regions;
- active support sizes of relevant projection fibers;
- BDD/ZDD representation size under an induced variable order;

than by raw counts `|Delta|` or `|C|` alone.

**Falsification:** benchmark families with small semantic ranks but persistent blowup unexplained by temporal formula size or fixed-point structure.

## C4 — Common finiteness principle behind ocLTL and CSL 2026

**Status:** OPEN-COMPARISON; do not assume true.

Investigate whether there is a useful general abstraction theorem subsuming both:

1. finite complete-type/orbit spaces from effective omega-categoricity; and
2. effective omega-regular satisfiability / omega-regular approximability of constraint sequences from CSL 2026.

**Falsification:** first seek separation examples. `(N,<)` already shows that omega-categoricity is not necessary for the CSL 2026 route.

**Questions:**

- Does effective omega-categoricity imply effective omega-regular satisfiability for an appropriate bounded constraint encoding?
- If not, what additional completion/homogeneity assumption is missing?
- Can omega-regular satisfiability hold for domains with infinite k-orbit spaces?
- Is there a categorical/automata object that both finite type abstraction and constraint-sequence automata instantiate?

## C5 — Decidability boundary for memory

**Status:** OPEN PROGRAM.

Map the exact boundary among bounded lookback, bounded registers, restricted recurrence, and unbounded freeze/register mechanisms.

**Known warning:** freeze LTL and unbounded register synthesis contain strong undecidability results; ocLTL deliberately fixes lookback; CSL 2026 deliberately fixes the number of registers.

**Falsification strategy:** attempt counter-machine encodings early, before proof construction.

## C7 — Symbolic representation classes preserved by synthesis operators

**Status:** OPEN PROGRAM; replaces the vague earlier "find a compressed representation" goal.

We now have one candidate representation: Boolean functions of ABA support bits, with a provably compact extension relation.

The next theorem target is:

> identify nontrivial classes of Tau/ABA formulas or games for which controllable predecessor and fixed-point iteration preserve a polynomial-size (in the support-bit dimension) ROBDD/ZDD/CNF representation.

**Attack plan:**

- exploit local/minterm variable incidence;

**Status:** CONJECTURE (parameterized algorithmics).

**Falsification:** produce low-`rho(C)` families where computing the partition or subsequent symbolic operations still require exponential dependence on raw constant count for unavoidable reasons.

## C9 — Generalize support Booleanization beyond ABA

**Status:** OPEN CONJECTURAL PROGRAM.

For another effectively omega-categorical structure M, seek a finite incidence-coordinate representation of k-types plus a compact extension relation from k to k+1 types, so FO quantification becomes symbolic projection without explicit type enumeration.

ABA succeeds because minterm supports have local independent refinement. The relevant general property may be stronger than omega-categoricity—possibly a form of effective free amalgamation / local extension factorization.

**Falsification-first strategy:** test equality and `(Q,<)` before proposing a general theorem. If their extension relations require qualitatively different coordinates, formulate a taxonomy rather than force a single abstraction.
