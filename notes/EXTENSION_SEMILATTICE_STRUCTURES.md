# Extension-semilattice synthesis across standard omega-categorical structures

**Status:** DERIVED comparative theorem schema with concrete standard examples; literature positioning checked against homogeneous-structure / CSP-polymorphism sources. The semilattice-on-extension-types viewpoint is a project abstraction, not claimed as established terminology.

The ABA results suggest a more general tractable mechanism than "support union" itself.

## 1. Fragment-specific extension semilattice

Fix a partial/base type sigma. Let E_sigma be the finite set of complete one-step response extension types relevant to a chosen synthesis fragment.

Suppose E_sigma carries a finite join-semilattice operation `vee` and the hard response constraints select either:

- a singleton legal set; or
- a nonempty subsemilattice L_sigma subseteq E_sigma.

Then the join

`top_L = vee { e : e in L_sigma }`

exists and belongs to L_sigma.

If the target predicate Good is upward with respect to the semilattice order,

`e <= e' and Good(e) => Good(e')`,

then

`exists e in L_sigma. Good(e)`

iff

`Good(top_L)`.

This is the abstract greatest-response theorem behind ABA's maximal support.

The important point is that the semilattice is **fragment-specific and type-level**. It need not arise from a global algebra polymorphism on the original structure.

## 2. Equality structure

Fix a tuple with b distinct parameter values `a_1,...,a_b`.

The one-point complete types are:

- `E_j`: y=a_j, for j=1..b;
- `F`: y is fresh / unequal to every a_j.

Define the star semilattice

- `E_j vee E_j = E_j`;
- `E_j vee E_k = F` for j!=k;
- `E_j vee F = F`;
- `F vee F = F`.

For conjunctions of equality/disequality literals:

- if a consistent equality `y=a_j` is required, the legal response set is the singleton `{E_j}`;
- otherwise only disequalities remove some E_j, while F remains legal. The remaining legal set is a subsemilattice with greatest element F.

The natural upward target fragment is generated by freshness/disequality predicates `y!=a_j`. Equality predicates themselves are not upward under this star order.

Thus the equality structure has an extremal response collapse for a nontrivial positive fragment even though its geometry is not ABA-like.

## 3. Dense linear order without endpoints

Fix distinct parameters

`a_1 < ... < a_b`.

The complete one-point types are the finite chain

`(-infinity,a_1), a_1, (a_1,a_2), a_2, ..., a_b, (a_b,+infinity)`.

Order them by position. Join is simply maximum.

Every nonempty legal subset of a finite chain has a greatest element, so every satisfiable conjunction of order/equality/disequality literals has a greatest response type.

For targets upward in the position order (for example generated by predicates of the form `y>a_j`), existential response search collapses to the greatest legal cut/type.

Dually, downward targets collapse to the least legal type.

Thus DLO supplies a chain-extremal synthesis mechanism rather than a Boolean-support one.

## 4. Rado/random graph: fresh-vertex fragment

Fix distinct old vertices `a_1,...,a_b` and restrict the response to a **fresh** vertex y.

A complete fresh one-point type is exactly its adjacency set

`N(y) subseteq {a_1,...,a_b}`.

The Rado extension property realizes every subset.

Hence the extension fiber is the Boolean cube

`P({a_1,...,a_b})`

with join = set union.

A conjunction of adjacency/nonadjacency literals requires

`R subseteq N(y) subseteq A\F`

for disjoint required-neighbor and forbidden-neighbor sets R,F. This legal family is a subsemilattice and has greatest response

`A\F`.

Every target upward in adjacency-set inclusion is therefore witnessed by that greatest fresh response whenever any response works.

This is the graph analogue of the ABA maximal-support theorem.

If equality-to-old-vertex responses are permitted as a separate branch, they should be handled as pinned singleton cases or by a richer fragment-specific extension order; do not silently identify them with fresh adjacency subsets.

## 5. Henson K_n-free graphs: exact obstruction

Let H_n be the countable universal homogeneous K_n-free graph, n>=3.

For a fresh response y over an old finite graph G, the neighbor set N(y) must not contain a K_(n-1), because y together with such a clique would form K_n.

Therefore admissible fresh neighborhoods form a downward-closed set family, but not generally a union-closed one.

### Minimal obstruction

Take an old `(n-1)`-clique

`C={v_1,...,v_(n-1)}`.

For n=3 this is simply an old edge `{a,b}`.

Each singleton `{v_j}` is an admissible fresh neighborhood. More generally many proper subsets of C are admissible.

But the union C itself is not admissible: a new vertex adjacent to all of C would create K_n.

Hence there are incomparable legal response types with no legal join/top under neighborhood inclusion.

### Synthesis consequence

For H_3 over an old edge a-b:

- response Q_a adjacent only to a witnesses the upward target `E(y,a)`;
- response Q_b adjacent only to b witnesses `E(y,b)`;
- no legal response witnesses their conjunction.

Thus

`CPre(W_a) intersect CPre(W_b)`

can strictly contain

`CPre(W_a intersect W_b)`.

The meet-preserving box collapse fails exactly where the greatest-response property fails.

This gives a clean separator between the Rado and Henson graph fragments.

## 6. General extremal-response theorem

Let each system response fiber over a partial type sigma have a finite preorder `(E_sigma,<=)`.

Suppose every satisfiable hard-constraint instance in fragment Gamma has a computable greatest legal response `g_sigma`, and suppose every target/operator predicate used by the positive temporal fragment is upward in `<=`.

Then for every upward target W,

`exists legal response e with W(e)`

iff

`W(g_sigma)`.

Therefore system existential choice can be compiled away and the synthesis predecessor becomes a universal/box-style predecessor on the environment side.

A semilattice whose legal sets are subsemilattices is one sufficient mechanism for obtaining the greatest element, but a total chain (DLO) or directly computable finite maximum is enough.

## 7. Relation to polymorphism theory

For relational omega-categorical CSPs, polymorphism identities are a central tractability tool. A genuine semilattice polymorphism is therefore a natural sufficient source of join closure in some response fragments.

However:

- the OrbitSynthesis condition is local to **extension types and a chosen positive fragment**;
- ABA's useful support union is not simply ordinary Boolean-algebra join acting as a polymorphism of the full BA signature;
- DLO's useful join is maximum on the finite extension-type chain, not a global DLO operation preserved by every relation;
- equality's star join is likewise a type-level construction.

So global polymorphism structure and extension-type semilattice structure should be compared, not conflated.

## 8. A potential classification program

For a homogeneous / omega-categorical structure M and a chosen response fragment Gamma:

1. compute complete one-point extension types over a finite base type;
2. identify a natural information/order structure on them;
3. determine whether every legal response fiber has a greatest element;
4. classify upward definable predicates in that order;
5. test whether the property is uniform/effective across base types;
6. derive the resulting positive-mu-calculus synthesis collapse if it holds;
7. construct the smallest forbidden configuration when it fails.

This gives a concrete theory-by-theory research program rather than the vague claim that omega-categoricity itself should imply efficient synthesis.
